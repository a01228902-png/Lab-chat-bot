# About the GDC TME Lab

The GDC TME Lab studies the tumor microenvironment (TME) to understand how
tumors interact with surrounding cells, immune cells, and signalling molecules.
Our goal is to translate these insights into better diagnostics and therapies.

## What is the tumor microenvironment?

The tumor microenvironment is the ecosystem of cells, blood vessels, signalling
molecules and extracellular matrix that surrounds a tumor. It plays a major role
in cancer growth, spread (metastasis), and response to treatment.

## What does the lab work on?

The lab focuses on single-cell sequencing, spatial transcriptomics, and immune
profiling of tumor samples. We combine wet-lab experiments with computational
analysis to map how cells communicate inside a tumor.
