# Frequently Asked Questions

## What are the lab hours?

The lab is open Monday to Friday from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. The lab is closed on
weekends and public holidays.

## How do I join the lab?

Prospective students and researchers should send their CV and a short statement
of interest to lab@example.org. We review applications on a rolling basis.

## What software do you use for analysis?

We mainly use Python and R for data analysis. Common tools include Scanpy,
Seurat, and custom pipelines for processing single-cell sequencing data.

## How is data stored and shared?

Raw and processed data are stored on the secure lab server with regular backups.
Approved collaborators can request access through the lab manager.

## How do I book lab equipment?

Equipment such as the microscope and sequencer can be booked through the shared
online calendar. Please cancel unused bookings so others can use the equipment.
